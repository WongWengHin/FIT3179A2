Behind the charts

Methods, sources & acknowledgements

ABS meat production: Quarterly ORIGINAL (not seasonally adjusted) ABS production is summed into calendar years. Cattle is beef and veal; sheep is mutton and lamb; poultry is chicken meat. Missing state quarters remain unavailable, not zero.

Specialisation: A state's percentage mix of cattle/sheep/pig meat is compared with Australia's equivalent red-meat mix. An index over 1 means relative specialisation. This does not measure livestock raised in that state.

Trends: Growth is calculated relative to the previous calendar year. Industry share is a national four-category percentage, and does not use the selected-state filter.

Regional charts: Ranks exclude missing state data. Dumbbell endpoints compare 2016 with the selected year. The year and category selectors apply only where appropriate.

ABARES economic value: Nominal gross values, agricultural totals and livestock carcass weights are from Agricultural commodities, September quarter 2026. The data uses July–June financial years, and FY 2025–26 values are estimates rather than final recorded outcomes.
