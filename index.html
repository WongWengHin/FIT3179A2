Behind the maps

Methods, sources & acknowledgements

ABS original quarterly meat output is summed by calendar year. Cattle combines beef and veal; sheep combines lamb and mutton. State counts are only included when all required quarterly observations are present. Missing values are not zero.

The specialisation map compares a state's internal mix of three red-meat categories with the national red-meat mix. A tile-grid map is schematic, not a geographical boundary map. The geographic data describes slaughter and processing, not animals reared locally.

All three diagrams are made with Vega-Lite. Readable specifications appear in charts/, and source-derived observations are in data/.
